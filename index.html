CIDR & SUBNET CALCULATOR

Works on IPv4 and IPv6 alike. Every number is computed with BigInt, so a /12 in IPv6 counts its addresses exactly rather than rounding to a float.

Host counts follow the usual convention: the network and broadcast addresses are not usable hosts, except on an IPv4 /31 (RFC 3021 point-to-point, both addresses usable) and a /32. IPv6 has no broadcast address, so every address in the block is usable.
